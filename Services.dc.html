Services

From the booking to the payslip.

01

Business Travel Management

We book and manage business trips for your employees, from a single train to Milan to a week of meetings abroad. Every booking is checked against your travel policy before it is confirmed.

What we handle
  • Flights, trains, hotels and car hire through one booking channel
  • Approval by the right manager before anything is paid
  • Negotiated rates with airlines, hotel groups and rental companies
  • Changes and cancellations handled by us, not by the traveler
  • One invoice per month, split by cost centre
What changes for you

You see what travel costs by person, department and route. Your travelers stop spending their evenings booking hotels.

02

Expense Management

Expense reports stop being a monthly chore. Employees photograph receipts as they go and the report builds itself.

What we handle
  • Receipt capture by phone, with the original archived digitally
  • Automatic checks against your spending limits and categories
  • Matching of company card transactions to receipts
  • VAT split out and ready for your accountant
  • Export to your accounting software
What changes for you

Reports approved in days instead of weeks, fewer errors, and VAT you can actually recover.

03

Reimbursement Management

When employees pay out of pocket or use their own car, they should be paid back correctly and on time. We calculate every reimbursement under Italian rules and pass it to payroll.

What we handle
  • Mileage reimbursement based on ACI tables
  • Daily allowances for trips in Italy and abroad
  • Out-of-pocket expenses, checked for traceable payment
  • Correct tax treatment under article 51 of the TUIR
  • A monthly file ready for your payroll provider
What changes for you

Employees are paid back in the next pay cycle, and every reimbursement holds up if the tax office asks.

04

Travel Risk Management

If your people travel for work, you are responsible for their safety while they do. We help you meet that duty with procedures that work at two in the morning, not only on paper.

What we handle
  • Risk checks on destinations before travel is approved
  • Pre-trip briefings for travelers going to higher-risk areas
  • A live record of who is travelling where
  • A 24-hour assistance line for your travelers
  • Incident procedures and insurance checks in line with D.Lgs. 81/2008
What changes for you

Documented duty of care, and a plan your team can follow when a flight is cancelled, a strike hits or a traveler falls ill.
