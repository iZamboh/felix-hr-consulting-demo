About

An HR consultancy for people who travel for work

Felix is based in Frosinone. We manage business travel, expenses, reimbursements and travel risk for companies of every size, from small local firms to groups with several sites.

Why we do this

One trip touches four offices

A business trip involves the person travelling, their manager, accounting and HR. Each of them usually has a separate process and a separate spreadsheet, and things get lost between them.

We started Felix to take that whole chain off our clients' hands. We sit between your people, your suppliers and your payroll, and we make sure each trip is booked, paid, reported and reimbursed once, correctly.

Because we are an HR consultancy, we also look at travel from the employee's side. People who travel for work should not lose money or evenings because of it.

How we work

Four things every client can count on

One contact person

You always know who to call. The same person handles your bookings, your questions and your monthly report.

Rules in writing

Every client gets a written travel and expense policy. If a rule isn't written down, we don't apply it.

Numbers every month

A monthly report on travel spend, expenses, reimbursements and open issues, short enough to read in ten minutes.

Italian rules, applied

ACI tables, daily allowances, VAT and duty of care. We keep track of the rules so your team doesn't have to.

Where we are

Based in Frosinone, between Rome and Naples

We meet clients in person across the region and work remotely with companies elsewhere in Italy. The travelers we look after go wherever the work takes them.
