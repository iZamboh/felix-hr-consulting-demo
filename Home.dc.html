What we do

Control and efficiency in a single solution.

Most companies we meet book trips by email, track expenses in spreadsheets and pay back employees at the end of the month, when someone finds the time. Costs are hard to see and people wait weeks for their money.

We put all of it into one managed flow. Trips are booked inside your policy, receipts are captured on the day, reimbursements reach payroll on schedule, and you know where your people are while they travel.
